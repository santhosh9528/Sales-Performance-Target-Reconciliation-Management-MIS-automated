"The company is generating strong revenue, but management believes that
profitability, collections, inventory efficiency, and customer performance
are deteriorating. Identify the exact problems and tell us what actions
should be taken."

1. WHY IS PROFITABILITY CHANGING?
=================================================================

FINDINGS:

Total Revenue        : ₹405,114,389.78
Total Profit         : ₹106,816,364.54
Overall Profit Margin: 26.37%

Although the company is generating strong revenue, profitability is not
consistent across products and categories.

Major reasons identified:

• Several products generate negative profit after considering discounts,
  returns and product cost.

• 14 products were identified as negative-profit Critical exceptions.

• Higher discounts are associated with lower profitability.
  Statistical analysis found a significant negative relationship between
  Discount % and Profit Margin.

  Correlation (r) : -0.5577
  P-value         : approximately 0
  Result          : Statistically significant

• Product returns are reducing realized profitability.

• Some categories operate at substantially lower margins.
  Electronics has a margin of approximately 20.46%, compared with
  Accessories at approximately 29.87%.

ACTION:

Management should introduce minimum-margin controls, review discount
policies, investigate negative-profit SKUs, reduce avoidable returns,
and monitor product-level net profitability instead of revenue alone.

=================================================================

FINDINGS:

Raw Records     : 23,947
Valid Records   : 23,642
Invalid/Removed : 305
Data Quality    : 98.73%

Overall data quality is high, but relational-integrity analysis
identified important master-data problems.

Candidate foreign-key audit identified 732 failed references across
transaction relationships.

Examples:

Sales -> Customer : 16
Sales -> Product  : 148
Sales -> Employee : 203
Sales Target -> Employee : 16
Order -> Customer : 14
Order -> Employee : 151
Invoice -> Order  : 8
Payment -> Invoice: 1
Purchase -> Product: 13
Inventory -> Product: 27
Attendance -> Employee: 132
Complaint -> Customer: 3

These are failed relationship references and should not be interpreted
as 732 unique bad rows because one transaction may contain more than
one invalid relationship.

ACTION:

• Enforce primary-key and foreign-key validation.
• Validate IDs before loading transactions.
• Introduce automated data-quality checks in Power Query/SQL.
• Maintain consistent master-data records.
• Reject or quarantine orphan transactions.
• Produce a data-quality exception report during each refresh.

=================================================================
MANAGEMENT CONCLUSION
=================================================================

The analysis confirms that the company's primary problem is not lack
of revenue.

The company generated approximately ₹405.11M in sales and ₹106.82M
in profit, but operational inefficiencies are creating substantial
business risk.

The most urgent concern is receivables management. Approximately
₹239.66M remains outstanding and ₹234.02M of this amount is more than
90 days old.

Inventory is the second major concern. Approximately ₹1.93B is tied
up in inventory, turnover is only 0.67, and 75 products are
overstocked while stockout days are also occurring.

Management should therefore prioritize:

Collections and credit control,
inventory optimization,
negative-profit product correction,
regional recovery,
supplier SLA improvement,
employee productivity,
customer complaint resolution,
and stronger data governance.

The automated MIS and Power BI dashboard should be used as the
central monitoring system for these KPIs and management exceptions.
